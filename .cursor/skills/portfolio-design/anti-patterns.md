# Anti-Patterns — NEVER Use

## Visual
- Purple-to-blue gradients on white backgrounds (the #1 AI slop indicator)
- Generic blob shapes or abstract gradient orbs
- Stock photo placeholders or random Unsplash-style imagery
- Rainbow gradient text
- Excessive glassmorphism / frosted glass everywhere
- Card grids where every card looks identical with no hierarchy

## Copy
- "Passionate developer"
- "Results-driven professional"
- "Leveraging cutting-edge technologies"
- "Bringing ideas to life"
- "Hi, I'm [Name] and I love to code"
- Lorem ipsum or placeholder text

## Typography
- Inter as the only font with no pairing
- All-caps body text
- More than 2 font families

## Layout
- Hero with floating laptop mockup (overused)
- Skills section as a boring bullet list with no visual treatment
- Contact section that's just an email with no personality

## Motion
- Parallax scrolling
- Bouncing animations
- Auto-playing carousels
- Cursor followers

## What TO Do Instead
- Use the user's actual project names and descriptions
- Pick ONE bold design choice (color, typography, or layout) and commit
- Write copy in first person, concise and specific
- Let projects be the visual focus
