# Color Palettes by Role

## Developer
- Background: `#0a0a0f` or `#111827`
- Text: `#f9fafb`
- Accent: `#22c55e` (green) or `#06b6d4` (cyan)
- Primary: `#1f2937`

## Designer
- Background: `#faf9f7` (warm white) or `#1a1a1a`
- Text: `#1a1a1a` or `#f5f5f5`
- Accent: `#e11d48` (rose) or `#f59e0b` (amber)
- Primary: `#374151`

## Creative
- Background: `#0f0f0f`
- Text: `#ffffff`
- Accent: `#ff6b35` or `#7c3aed` (use sparingly)
- Primary: `#1a1a2e`

## Minimal
- Background: `#ffffff` or `#0a0a0a`
- Text: `#171717` or `#fafafa`
- Accent: single color — `#2563eb` or `#000000`
- Primary: same as background with subtle borders

## Editorial
- Background: `#fffef9` (cream)
- Text: `#1c1917`
- Accent: `#92400e` (warm brown)
- Primary: `#44403c`

## Rules

- Max 3 colors in palette (bg, text, accent)
- Accent used only for CTAs, links, and highlights
- Ensure WCAG AA contrast minimum
